

### 📊 Data Analyst | Finance & Business Controlling Specialist

With over 10 years of experience in Finance and Business Controlling, having worked at companies such as ZF do Brasil, Sorocaba Refrescos (Coca-Cola bottler), and Grupo Petrópolis, I bring a strong business perspective and financial expertise to Data Analytics.

I combine my background in financial management, KPI analysis, and business performance with technical skills in data analysis, helping transform data into actionable insights and support better business decisions.

**Business & Finance:** SAP, Advanced Excel, VBA, Power Query, Budgeting, Forecasting, KPI Analysis, Cost Management, Financial Reporting
